Privacy Policy

Brahma Coin · BRAHMNETWORK · public launch

This page summarizes how a BRAHMNETWORK node operator and the public host may process limited technical data. Full text: project file docs/PRIVACY_POLICY.md. Replace the contact below before regulated use.

What we may process

  • IP address, User-Agent, and TLS metadata — security and rate limits
  • RPC requests you send — to execute ledger methods
  • Join memo dev:<phoneId>[:referrer] — one claim per phone (anti-farm)
  • Public addresses and on-chain amounts — ledger data is public by design
  • APK download logs — when the operator serves /wallet.apk

What we do not do here

  • No KYC identity collection in the open-source wallet
  • No intended sale of personal data for advertising in the default config
  • Do not put names or secrets in transaction memos

Blockchain notice

Sealed transactions are public and hard to erase. Assume permanence.
